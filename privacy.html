Privacy Policy

Facebook Clip AI is a browser-based creator tool. Meta App ID and Worker URL settings may be stored in your browser. Facebook access tokens are used to list Pages you manage and publish content you request. This static site does not intentionally sell Facebook user data or use it for advertising.

Video processing is performed in your browser. When you publish, the finished Reel and the Page token needed for that request are transmitted over HTTPS to the configured Worker and Meta/Facebook services. The included Worker does not intentionally persist the Page token or uploaded video after the request completes.

You can disconnect Facebook by using the Disconnect button and revoke the app separately in your Facebook settings.
